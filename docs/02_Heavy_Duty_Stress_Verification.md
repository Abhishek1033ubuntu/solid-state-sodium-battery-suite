# Heavy-Duty Stress Verification & Thermal Safety Analysis

## 1. Stress Scenario Protocols
The full-cell model was subjected to a combined severe overstress protocol:
1. **Thermal Ramp:** Temperature increase from $25^\circ\text{C}$ to $355^\circ\text{C}$ over $60\text{ seconds}$[cite: 4].
2. **Electrical Overcharge:** $15\text{C}$ continuous overcharge pulse[cite: 4].
3. **Volumetric Strain:** $8\%$ structural deformation[cite: 4].

## 2. Dynamic Results & Pressure Response
* **Liquid Li-Ion Baseline:** Thermal runaway onset at $180^\circ\text{C}$ ($t = 28\text{ s}$), leading to organic solvent boiling, structural oxygen release, and explosive pressure ($>10^6\text{ Bar}$)[cite: 4].
* **Solid-State Sodium Matrix:** Covalent $\text{P–O}$ bonds ($590\text{ kJ/mol}$) prevent oxygen release[cite: 4]. Internal cell pressure remains under $6\text{ Bar}$, well below the $50\text{ Bar}$ burst limit[cite: 4].
