# Full-Cell Electrochemistry & Material Doping Mechanisms

## 1. Cathode Matrix ($\text{Na}_{3.2}\text{V}_{1.8}\text{Zr}_{0.2}(\text{PO}_4)_2\text{F}_2 \cdot \text{C}_{\text{graphene}}$)
* **Inductive Effect via Fluorination:** Electronegative $\text{F}^-$ substitution increases ionic character across $\text{V–O/F}$ bonds, lowering Vanadium $d$-orbital energies and shifting operating potential from $3.4\text{ V}$ to $4.1\text{ V}$[cite: 4].
* **Bottleneck Expansion via Zr-Doping:** Radius expansion ($\text{Zr}^{4+}$) widens 3D NASICON interstitial pathways to $2.85\text{ \AA}$, dropping hopping activation energy below $0.18\text{ eV}$[cite: 4].

## 2. Solid-State Electrolyte ($\text{Na}_{3.1}\text{Zr}_{1.95}\text{Hf}_{0.05}\text{Si}_2\text{PO}_{12}$)
* Eliminates liquid carbonate flammability.
* Provides high bulk ionic conductivity ($\sigma_i \approx 2.4 \times 10^{-2}\text{ S/cm}$) while acting as a mechanical dendrite barrier[cite: 4].

## 3. Copper-Free 3D MXene Anode
* Replaces heavy copper current collectors with lightweight sodiated MXene scaffolds, reducing cell dead weight to achieve $745.5\text{ Wh/kg}$[cite: 4].
